You can count on it

Every listing is checked first.

We read each happy hour before it appears, so what you see is what the place is really running.

Real, licensed places

Every place with a listing holds a current Texas alcohol permit, checked against the state’s public records.

Read by a person

A person at Zambux reads the specials before you see them, and again if the wording changes.

Fresh, never stale

Places renew their listing at least every 30 days, or it disappears.

Prices end by 11 pm

Reduced drink prices can’t run past 11 pm in Texas, so a listing can’t be set to.

No gimmicks

Listings with unlimited or free-drink offers, coupons or cover charges are blocked before they can be posted.

The tab asks you to confirm your age, and every listing reminds you to drink responsibly.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? support@zambux.com

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one. The Happy hours tab opens once you’ve created an account and confirmed you’re 21 or older. Browsing regular deals doesn’t need an account.

How do I get the discount?

You don’t claim anything. A happy hour is the business’s own special at its own location. Zambux just tells you where and when. Prices and times can change, so check with the business.

Is it only for restaurants?

For now, yes, and only in Austin. Bars and restaurants with a Texas alcohol permit.

Does Zambux sell alcohol?

No. Zambux doesn’t sell or deliver alcohol. It lists happy hours that businesses run themselves.

My favorite place isn’t listed.

Use “Suggest a place” in the app, or write to support@zambux.com, and we’ll invite them.
