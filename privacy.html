Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Draft. This text is a plain-language starting point and is being reviewed by a lawyer. It is not final and not yet in effect.

What we collect

Your email address and password (the password is handled securely by our login provider and we can't read it). Whether you are a customer, a business owner or staff. For businesses: name, address, phone, website, description, logo, opening hours and team members' email addresses. For everyone: the zambux you grab and redeem, and when. If you allow it, your location while you use the app.

How we use it

To run Zambux: sign you in, show deals near you, create and scan codes, show businesses how their zambux perform, prevent fraud and keep the service safe. We use your location only on your phone to show distances and the map. We don't store it on our servers.

Who sees what

Businesses see the code, the time and the deal for each grab. They don't see your email address or name. Staff you are invited by can see the business they work for. Anyone can see a business's public details and live zambux.

Who helps us run Zambux

We use service providers to store data and send email, such as Supabase for our database and login, and Apple for maps and address lookup. They handle data only to provide those services for us. We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Account, then Delete my account). That removes your profile and sign-in, your grabs, and, for a business, its deals and team. Backups may hold copies for a short time afterwards.

Your choices

You can turn location off in your phone's Settings and the app will measure distances from downtown Austin instead. You can ask us to correct or export your data at support@zambux.net.

Children

Zambux is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will say so in the app. Questions about your data: support@zambux.net.
