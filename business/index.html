The idea

Empty seats pay nothing.

Every local business has slow hours: an empty table at 2 p.m., an open bay on a Tuesday, an unbooked chair. That time can’t be saved and sold later. Hotels have always known this, so they fill rooms at a discount rather than leave them empty. Zambux brings that thinking to every local business, in real time.

Happy hours

Tell people when you’re pouring.

If you run a regular happy hour, post it once: days, hours and specials. Customers near you can find it in the app, with no cut of your sales. It’s an advertisement only, so there’s nothing for customers to grab or scan.

  • Included with the Starter and Pro plans
  • Needs your Texas (TABC) permit number, which we check against TABC’s records
  • We read each listing before customers see it
  • Runs up to 30 days at a time, and we remind you 5 days before it ends
  • Prices end by 11 pm. No unlimited or free-drink offers, coupons or cover charges

You’re responsible for following Texas TABC rules. These checks help, but they don’t replace your own compliance.

Questions

From owners.

Something else? support@zambux.com

How long does verification take?

We check each business by hand, and we aim to finish within 48 hours of you sending your details.

Does Zambux take a cut of my sales?

No. Customers grab for free and pay you directly at your counter, at the price you set. We never take a share of your sales and charge no fee per redemption. Your only cost is your monthly plan, and the Free plan costs nothing.

What if too many people come?

You set a limit on every deal, and you can stop new grabs any time. Deals also end by themselves, within 4 hours.

How do my staff scan?

Staff sign in to the app, open the scanner, and scan each customer’s code. Staff can scan but can’t change your deals or settings.

Can I end a zambux early?

Yes. Stop new grabs, or close the deal, from the app any time.

Is my city open?

We’re starting in Austin, Texas. More cities are coming, and the app tells you whether yours is open when you sign up.
