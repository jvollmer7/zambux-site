The idea

Empty seats pay nothing.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it cost customers anything?

No. Grabbing a zambux is free and creates no obligation. You only pay the discounted price at the business, if you decide to use it.

What does "grab" mean?

It's how you save a deal for yourself. You get a code. When you arrive, the business scans it and your coupon starts. If you can't make it, you can let it go so someone else gets the spot.

How long do deals last?

A few hours at most, and only while the business is open. Once the staff scan your code, your coupon is good for 4 hours or until closing, whichever comes first. If a deal ends before you get there, you still get a 10 minute window to be scanned.

Why can't I grab more than one at a time?

It keeps deals fair. Holding one at a time means spots go to people who will actually show up. You can switch to a different one whenever you like, and once you've been scanned you're free to grab another.

Are the deals really half off?

Yes. The app won't let a business post anything below 50% off. Percent-off deals are 50, 60, 70 or 80 percent, and buy one get one free works out to exactly half. If a deal isn't as advertised, customers can report it.

What kinds of businesses can post?

Any local business with slow hours: restaurants and cafes first, then salons, spas, fitness, home services and more. Zambux deals don't include alcohol.

How does a business get verified?

We check each business before it can post: that it's real, local and who it says it is. It's a quick process and part of what keeps deals trustworthy.

What does it cost a business?

You can start on the Free plan at no cost and post up to 2 zambux a month. Paid plans with more room will come later.
