Questions

Good to know.

Still wondering something? Write to support@zambux.com.

How do I use a zambux?

Tap Grab it in the app, then get to the business before the deal ends. They scan your code when you arrive, and your coupon starts. If the deal ends first, you get a 10 minute window to be scanned.

What if I get there and it’s over?

A deal is live until its timer runs out. If you grabbed it and it ends before you arrive, you have 10 minutes to be scanned. After that your spot goes back, so aim to arrive before the timer ends.

Can I grab more than one?

One at a time. Use it, or let it go, then grab another. That keeps deals fair for everyone.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone for now. Android is next if enough people ask. Android users can leave an email on the Get the app page and we’ll write once, when it’s ready.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The app is free and grabbing is free. You only pay the business the discounted price when you’re there.
