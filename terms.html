What Zambux is

Zambux connects local businesses with customers. Businesses post time-limited offers called zambux. Customers grab them for free and redeem them at the business. Zambux does not sell the food, drinks or services in an offer. The business does, and the business is responsible for them.

Accounts

You must be at least 13 to make a customer account and at least 18 to make a business account. Keep your login private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Give us accurate information.

Grabbing and redeeming

Grabbing a zambux is free and creates no obligation. You redeem it when the business scans your code. Once scanned, your coupon works for 4 hours or until the business closes, whichever comes first. A zambux has no cash value, can't be combined or resold, and is limited to one per customer. Some zambux have a limited number of spots. You can let go of a grab you haven't redeemed to free the spot. If a zambux ends before you are scanned, you have 10 more minutes to be scanned.

Rules for businesses

Post only offers you will honor, with accurate wording, and follow every law that applies to them, including health and advertising rules. Offers can't include alcohol. Offers for health services need approval from Zambux first. When customers have grabbed a zambux you must honor it on the terms they grabbed, even if you edit the offer later, and you can't end it early. You can invite staff to scan codes. You are responsible for what your staff do. Zambux may verify your business and may remove an offer or an account that breaks these terms.

Plans

Businesses can use a free plan with limits on how many zambux they post and how many run at once. Paid plans may be offered later and will be described before you buy them.

What not to do

Don't share, copy or sell codes. Don't create fake grabs or fake businesses. Don't scrape the app, try to break it, or use it to harass anyone. We may suspend accounts that do.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. A business can't be deleted while customers still hold grabs or coupons that it promised. Deleting your account removes your data as described in our Privacy Policy.

No guarantees and limits of liability

Zambux is provided as is. We don't guarantee that offers are accurate, available or good, or that the app will always work. Disagreements between a customer and a business are between them. To the extent the law allows, Zambux isn't liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability for any claim is limited to $100.
